# Visual critic — final review (round 6)

Independent subagent, judged from renders only (`shots/critic6`: 6 desktop poses at 3456×2234 with 1:1 crops,
two 6-frame consecutive bursts + 8 sparse drive frames, 3 mobile-tier renders).

**Verdict: highest severity present is MINOR; nothing above MINOR. No BLOCKER, MAJOR or MODERATE.**

## Remaining MINOR items (priority order; 1–3 closest to MODERATE)
1. Chase camera lets a trail lamp post pass right in front of the lens (sparse-02).
2. Windshield shows a hard zigzag / speckled patches that read as a glitch rather than a reflection.
3. Snow in full shade is a fairly featureless blue-grey; groomer lacks contact darkening there.
4. Spruce very close to the camera reads as paper cut-outs (flat branch cards, sawtooth edges).
5. Spruce foliage in midday shade is almost black.
6. Mountain rock patches are soft smudges; a few boulders on the ridge look out of scale; stipple on mobile.
7. Little aerial perspective in the mountain vista.
8. Distant forest reads as a uniform instanced carpet.
9. Bog pine saplings do not read as pine.
10. Bloom too hot on track cleats / tiller housing at golden hour; tiller flap looks semi-transparent.
11. Sun-fleck shadow edges are grainy at 1:1 (stable frame to frame).
12. Faceted terrain / stepped berm edge around fresh grooming at 1:1.
13. Tiller spray is weak (a few hard dots); exhaust barely visible.
14. Cabin sits too cleanly on the terrain; chimney smoke is a faint blob.
15. Unexplained pink/mauve flat patches on snow at far right of the cabin shot (unchecked).
16. Old trail's right half buried under a smooth drift with a hard edge in the forest shot.
17. Mobile portrait: top buttons on a second row; TRACKS smaller than TILLER; tight bottom margin.

NIT: opaque DRIVE pad disc, plain lamp posts and blade, footprint-like dabs in golden-hour snow, blurry midday cloud.
