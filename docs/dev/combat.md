# Combat, driving and the game loop

`combat.js`: shells, damage, enemy AI and roles, waves, reload, enemy detection and gunnery, the tank's driving and fire control. Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **`combat.js`:** everything about fighting, preallocated in `initCombat`: the shell pool (APFSDS, HEAT-MP, HE, coax MG, enemy 125 mm and 30 mm, smoke grenades; shell types 0–12 are listed above `TRACE`, 12 being the RPG rocket of the infantry) with ballistic flight and sub-stepped hits against terrain, buildings (`C.bld.at`, real roof height), circle colliders (`collAt` grid) and vehicle hit boxes; building damage and collapse effects (`bldHit`, `bldState`: dust cloud, debris chunks, a few seconds of fire for HE and the drone, scorch, shake, `C.sfx('collapse')`); damage by zone and facing; explosions, muzzle blast, tracers and flashes (`fx` glow sprites plus the `l2` flash light); smoke clouds that block line of sight; enemy AI (follow trails towards the player, pushed out of buildings and colliders through `C.solid`, line of sight through terrain, intact buildings, canopy raster and smoke, lead and aim error, reload); waves, resupply, score, the minimap and the HUD pills.
- **Driving and aiming:** one momentum physics for the tank (faster on plowed roads than in deep snow, pivot steer, brakes that hold on slopes, suspension squat and recoil rocking). The mouse moves a stabilised sight line; a terrain ray march gives the aim point and range, and the turret and gun slew at their own rates towards the ballistic solution for the loaded round. Cameras: commander view (0), gunner sight (1), drone (4). Mode 3 (free-fly) still exists in `main.js` but has no player control any more (removed as a cheat; the F key and `api.fly` are gone). The reticle, gun marker with reload arc, hit marker, sight field stop and damage flash are drawn in `post.wgsl` (`fsFinal`), so the HUD never writes to the DOM per frame.
- **Reload (`RELOAD` in `combat.js`):** 4 s for the Leopard's hand-loaded 120 mm (6 s at first; the owner was overwhelmed by several enemies at once and asked what is realistic: a trained loader manages roughly 4–6 s from the ready rack, the T-72's autoloader about 7–8 s, which is its 8.5 s here). A change of ammunition also costs one reload.
- **Enemy detection and gunnery (`SEEK`, `AIMK` in `combat.js`):** the owner was overwhelmed by several enemies at once and asked for both to come down. `SEEK` = 0.6 scales how fast a crew picks the tank up (the positive term of `e.see`; soldiers have the same 0.6 in `infantry.js`): a T-72 with a clear line detects a still tank in 2.5 / 3 s at 150 / 250 m (1.5 / 2 s before) and a still tank in the open is not detected beyond about 1400 m (1900 m before). `AIMK` = 1.4 scales the gunnery error: measured on the lake over 120 shots, a T-72 hits a still tank with 51 % of its rounds at 200 m (82 % before) and 44 % at 300 m (69 %). RPG error went from 0.5 m + 1.2 % of the range to 0.7 m + 1.6 %. The Kornet is unchanged (it is guided).
- **Game loop:** six waves, resupply and partial repair between waves, then a victory card and endless play. Each wave is a scenario (`planWave` in `combat.js`): wave 1 'Recon in force' from the north, waves 2–5 a shuffled bag of 'Tank platoon with escort', 'BMP raid on the roads', 'Pincer' and 'Two echelons', wave 6 'Final assault'. The main axis changes every wave (picked among bearings that have trails or roads at 500–1000 m, at least about 60° from the last one); groups can arrive later (`PQ` pending spawns, by time or when few are left) and are announced with direction and range. Roles per vehicle (`e.role`): assault (closes to a stand-off range), overwatch (drives to a high spot with a view, `pickSpot`, and holds), flanker (swings wide to the side first), raider (BMP, fast along the plowed roads `W.roads`). All of them shoot and scoot, pop smoke and back off when hit, keep station in a column, slide along walls and escape after repeated stuck events (`G.stuck` counts them). `?peace` (also implied by `auto`, `perf` and the fantasy world) disables the waves. The menu row Peace mode (`#mP`, `api.peace`, `localStorage.gpeace`) does the same at run time: it sets `game.peace`, restarts, and is remembered; the fantasy world is always free roam.

## Gotchas
- A vehicle hit box is thin where hull and turret meet; main-gun shells are sub-stepped every 0.6 m (MG 1.5 m). Coarser steps let rounds pass through the roofline.
- The sight ray ranges on a generous sphere around each enemy (`aimHit`), so a displayed range does not mean the round will hit; scripted tests should aim with `__aimAt` at a point inside the hull.
- Trees do not stop projectiles; enemy line of sight uses the canopy raster (`nrm` B), which does not change when a tree goes over. Enemy vehicles leave no tracks in the mask.
