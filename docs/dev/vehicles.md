# Vehicles and models

The Leopard, the running gear and track loops, T-72, BMP, Tigr, BTR, buggy, motorcycle and their crews, vehicle materials, MX3000 metro units. Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **`leopard.js`, `gear.js`:** the player's Leopard 2A4NO (`buildLeopard`, `LEO`; about 25 000 triangles: welded turret shell from a plan (`shell`, 3.2 m wide, roof 2.46 m) with the EMES 15 seated in a notch in the right cheek, PERI R17, cupola, MG3 on a skate rail, discharger cups, bustle basket, wedge mantlet set back between the cheeks, skirts (the first heavy module cut away over the idler), engine deck, rear louvre band, Rh-120 with sleeve and fume extractor) and the shared running gear (`runningGear(b, type, colour, hi)`). **Track loops:** `GEAR` holds per tracked vehicle (0 Leopard, 1 T-72, 2 BMP) the road wheels, idler, sprocket and link thickness; `trackLoop` turns that into the convex hull of four circles (4 straight runs, 4 arcs: ground run, rise to the sprocket, return run, fall from the raised idler), `loopAt` evaluates it, and `TRACK_WGSL` is the same table as WGSL constants (`TRKL`, `TRKT`) that `main.js` puts in front of `objdeform.wgsl`, so the animated link bars (`MAT.bar`, pos = (x, along, outward), uv = (loop position, side), last vertex float = vehicle type) follow the same path as the belt mesh. The road wheels stand on the links (link outer face = ground line, y 0). The whole running gear is part 3: `tankParts` writes a fourth matrix without the hull's suspension rocking (`dp`, `dr`), so the links stay on the ground when the hull squats (wheeled vehicles overwrite part 3 with their first axle). `TRK` only holds the track centre line and width now (stamps, physics). Mesh helpers: `face`, `cbox` (chamfered box), `plate`, `lathe` (with `uvr` it writes polar uv for `MAT.wheel`: bolt ring, dish dirt, matt black tyre beyond 0.76 of the radius), `pipe`, `fixWinding` (helpers only guarantee normals; `fixWinding` makes the winding follow them). `MAT.camo` paints whitewash as strokes: two sets of capsules on jittered grids (broad swabs, a sparser set crossing them), each with its own angle, length and width, ragged edges and bristle streaks, 0.6 coverage at the front falling to 0.42 at the sooty rear; every face is painted in its own plane, chosen by a hard switch on the part-space normal (`nlp`, from derivatives of `lp` taken at the top of `fs` and weighted so a 45 degree chamfer does not flicker between two planes); the vertex green channel seeds the strokes (`tint` in `leopard.js`; keep `fract(g * 9000 + 0.37)` away from an integer or the seed flips per pixel). Vehicle materials (20, 21, 8, 14) get a strong fill term for light bounced off the snow (one line after the main lighting sum in `object.wgsl`): without it the shaded side was a black silhouette (shadowed olive 0.005 displayed luminance, now about 0.03-0.045). Dark parts need a very low specular (0.004-0.012): with more, the sun term turns albedo 0.03 nearly white. The turret bustle and its basket sweep the engine deck at 1.81 m, so nothing on the deck may stand higher. `node tools/leocheck.mjs` (no GPU) prints triangle counts, bounds per part, the Leopard's proportions and the loop figures, and fails on NaN or a winding that disagrees with the normals; `tools/vehshots.mjs` renders the look-check views, including the Leopard on bare ice and a drive / pivot / reverse check of the links (`--q therm=1` adds query parameters).
- **`tank.js`:** the other tracked vehicles (`buildT72`, `buildBMP`; it re-exports `buildLeopard`) and `tankParts`, which writes the part matrices (0 hull, 1 turret, 2 gun). The tracked vehicles share one running gear (`gear.js`). The per-vehicle constants (`LEO`, `T72`, `BMP`) hold the turret ring, trunnion, muzzle and hit-box sizes. `tankParts` uses shared scratch matrices, so `main.js` copies the player's into `hullM` / `turM` / `gunM` before the enemies are updated.
- **T-72B3 (`buildT72` in `tank.js`):** about 17 400 triangles (18 500 with the cope cage, `vehshots --cage`): one smooth dome turret with eight Kontakt-5 wedges, Sosna-U box, Luna, cupola with NSVT, Tucha tubes, glacis ERA, fender bins and fuel cells, skirts with ERA on the front third, log, fuel drums, snorkel. Its road wheels are still evenly spaced (`runningGear` spaces them from n, z0, z1).
- **Light and wheeled vehicles (phase 4):** `ETYPE` now has six types (`T72T BMPT TIGRT BTRT BUGT MOTOT` = 0–5): T-72B3, BMP-2, Tigr-M with a Kornet (`buildTigr`), BTR-82A (`buildBTR`), an assault buggy with a PKM (`buildBuggy`) and a motorcycle team (`buildMoto`); `MAXE` = 14. Each type carries its own `K` (in `tank.js`) with hit-box half-extents `bx`/`bz`, a collision circle `cr` at ±`cs`, and for wheeled ones `wr` and axle positions `ax`: `wheelParts` writes one rolling part per axle (parts 3+, both wheels on it), after `tankParts`. Per type: `mgd` (damage per coax/hatch MG round: BTR 0.15, Tigr 0.5, buggy 6, bike 8, BMP 3), `turn` (yaw rate; fast wheeled vehicles need more to hold an 8 m route point), `score`, `sfx`, muzzle size, spread and minimap marker (`map`: T-72 circle, BMP/BTR square, Tigr yellow diamond, buggy/bike dot). The light ones have no smoke dischargers and a short `hold` (they rush in). Enemy engine pitch per type in `audio.js` (`ERATE`). **Kornet** (shell 8): 250 m/s, life 10 s; `guide()` keeps it on the tank (pure pursuit at 2.2/s, the motor cancels gravity) while the launcher still sees the tank (`losVis`, every 4th step), so smoke or cover cuts it, and it then veers 0.06–0.13 rad off its line; a smoke-grey trail; 38/50/62 hull by facing; launch toast and the HUD arrow (missiles first). **Enemy MG** (shell 9): 0.3 a hit on the Leopard. Scenarios: **Motorised raid** (3–5 buggies and bikes down the roads, a BTR, a Tigr on overwatch, a T-72 later from wave 4) and **ATGM ambush** (two Tigrs on overwatch, a BTR or BMP screen, two T-72s flanking later); the shuffled bag now holds six scenarios, and the BMP raid sometimes sends BTRs. **Roads and trails (`ETYPE.road`):** the wheeled ones (Tigr, BTR, buggy, bike) are road-bound (2) and the BMP prefers roads (1); a plowed road and a groomed ski trail count the same for them. They spawn on one (never where canopy > 90), `findRoute` looks for a road or trail up to 240 m off before driving cross-country, off a route `forestDir` bends their heading by up to ±1 rad towards the least canopy 20–45 m ahead (every 0.5 s), and their overwatch, scoot and escape spots need canopy < 45 (`canLim`). `tools/roadtest.mjs` measures it per type (Motorised raid, BMP raid, ATGM ambush).
- **Tigr and BTR (`src/wheeled.js`), shared helpers (`src/vehutil.js`):** `buildTigr` (about 12 600 triangles: tub with real wheel wells, Tigr-M nose with a low wide louvred grille and round lamps outboard, raked two-pane windscreen in bolted frames, tumblehome, underbody with frame, differentials and wishbones, Kornet mount with sight and two 1.3 m containers; `TIGR.wr` is 0.54) and the shared `tyre()` (lathed carcass, lugs, rim, nuts; optional ninth parameter `{ nuts, lugs, ctis, seg }`); `buildBTR` (about 15 950 triangles, tyres at `seg: 16`): a BTR-82A as a tub between four trapezoid wheel arches with inclined lower side plates (`sill`), lofted bow and stern, sloped upper sides, glacis with the folded trim vane, two windscreens with raised shutters, two-leaf side doors between axles 2 and 3, firing ports and vision blocks, silencers on the rear upper sides, engine-deck louvres, water-jet cover, and the low BPPU cone turret with the 2A72 in an external cradle (bore 0.13 m above the trunnion), PKTM, sight box, searchlight and smoke dischargers. Russian paint is `MAT.rus` 21: dark matt olive, streaks, slush low down, crisp-edged snow pads on upward faces; a vertex with `uv.x = -9` takes no snow (warm plates). Vehicle paint, links and wheels (materials 20, 21, 8, 14) get a snow-bounce fill light in `object.wgsl` (stronger than physical, an art fix for black shadow sides).
- **Buggy and motorcycle models (`src/bikes.js`):** `buildBuggy` (tube space frame with a ring in the main hoop, wishbones and coil-overs, rear flat four with one transverse silencer, bucket seats, spare wheel on the rear stays, narrower front than rear tyres; about 17.5k triangles) and `buildMoto` (enduro: raked telescopic fork with gaiters, wire-spoked knobbly wheels in the x = 0 plane, tube frame, finned single, high exhaust, rack with a roll and two canvas panniers; about 10.3k triangles). Helpers there: `tube` / `pipe` (swept tubes, filleted corners), `lathe` (revolution about x; a doubled profile point is a hard edge), `plate`, `beam`, `knob`, `band`, `xform`, `snow` (a `MAT.snow` pillow: the vehicle materials take no settled snow in the shader), `lamp`. **The riders are the infantry model** (`buildSoldier(0, 8)`: the second argument is the segment count round the limbs) posed once at load and baked in: `pose(...)` fills the soldier pose parameters in vehicle space (`RIG` and `S.place` are exported from `soldier.js` for this), `bakeRider(b, { rifle, pack, toe, up, straps })` solves it with the soldier IK, levels the soles (the model's ankle is rigid), drops the pack where a seat back is, tones the overall down to a warm grey-white (about 0.62) and darkens seat, inner thighs, knees, cuffs and undersides in the vertex colours (the object shader has no occlusion, so white cloth in shade looked self-lit), adds shoulder caps that run into the torso, harness straps and goggles. In the thermal sight `object.wgsl` gives materials 24–28 on a vehicle body temperatures. **Buggy crew:** driver and passenger (AK upright between his knees) reclined in the seats, and a gunner standing on the floor behind them in the ring, leaning on a webbing sling: his pelvis and legs are part 0 and his torso, head and arms are part 1 (`up: 1`), so he twists at the waist with the gun (his waist is 5 cm off the yaw axis; past about 120° the twist is more than a body can do). The PKM has no stock (the gunner's chest is where it would be) and rides on a carriage on the ring (part 1); the gun is part 2 and pitches about `BUGGY.gy/gz`, 0.4 m behind the visible yoke, so the yoke and the hands slip a few centimetres at full elevation. **Motorcycle:** parts 1 and 2 are empty: their pivot (`MOTO.tz`, `gy`) lies in the rider, so a rifle in them left the pillion's hands; the pillion is twisted to the right in the infantry's standing aim (`shouldered(yaw)`: butt in the shoulder, cheek weld), firing 40° off the axis past the driver. The cloth shader's worn-patch noise is seeded by the vertex red, which the baked shading varies, so on vehicles (`isG`) the seed is dropped (it showed as mosaic squares). Wheel treads use `b.at(..., 0, a)` (pitch +a) so the local y axis is radial. `tools/vehshots.mjs ... --aim` adds shots with the weapon traversed and laid on the player, `--q therm=1` passes a query.
- **Motorcycle pillion gunner:** pelvis and legs in part 0, torso in part 1, head, arms and AK in part 2; `MOTO.tz` 0.64 / `gy` 1.54 / `gz` 0.59 / `mz` -0.3 put the pivot at his spine and the fire point on the muzzle. `MOTO.ya` gives the shown yaw arcs (34°–149° left, 34°–97° right): `visYaw()` in `combat.js` keeps a per-enemy shown yaw `e.vy` so he never swings through the driver (the shots still follow the true `turYaw`), passed to `tankParts(out, t, K, yaw)`. Any older sentence below about empty parts 1/2 or a fixed 40° pose is superseded.
- **Wrecks of open vehicles:** the riders baked into the buggy and motorcycle meshes are tagged uv.x = -8 (`bakeRider`), and `objdeform.wgsl` collapses those vertices once the vehicle's burnt value (`OB.tr.z`) is above 0, in the main and shadow passes; `kill()` in `combat.js` calls `inf.bodies(x, z, yaw, n)` (3 for a buggy, 2 for a bike), which lays that many dead soldiers beside the wreck (no extra score). Before, the charred crew sat upright on the wreck (the owner's report).
- **MX3000 metro units (`mxCar` and the `mx*` helpers in `props.js`):** lofted shell with tumblehome and rounded roof, flush rounded-trapezoid cab screen, three plug doors a side, bellows, roof units under a snow blanket, underfloor boxes, bogies with collector shoes on the conductor-rail side (`t.s3`); about 16 000 triangles per unit. `MAT.tglass` 31 is tinted glass over a ray-cast interior (seats, poles, far windows; `col.b = 1` = cab glass with sky reflection), `MAT.coach` 32 the satin body paint that holds its albedo back where it faces the sun (it clipped to white otherwise). `platFit()` in `world.js` fits every platform top to its track's rail head + 1.05 m as a sloping plane 8 cm off the car side (level boarding; tops moved up to +0.49 m against lidar; the collision record is stepped boxes).
