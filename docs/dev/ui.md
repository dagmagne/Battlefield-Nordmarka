# Controls, HUD, map and language

Controls (keyboard, touch, gamepad, drone views and OSD), minimap and big map, language (`lang.js`), time of day. Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **Controls:** WASD drive, mouse aim (click captures the pointer), LMB or Enter fire, RMB hold or C for the gunner sight (wheel changes magnification), Space MG (coax, or the hatch MG when aiming high or at a drone), Q force the hatch MG, 1–3 ammunition, G smoke grenades, K camouflage net, V thermal sight, X drone, Z drone type, B artillery fire mission, T time of day, N snow, M sound, U music, R restart, H help, Esc menu. Gamepad (`pollPad` in `ui.js`), tank: left stick drive, right stick aim, R2 fire, L2 sight (hold; L3 latches it), R1 MG, R3 force hatch MG, L1 smoke, □ ammo, △ thermal, hold ✕ drone, hold ○ artillery, D-pad ↑↓ magnification, ←→ drone type, Create big map, Options menu. Drone (DJI Mode 2): left stick height and yaw, right stick forward/back and sideways, L1/L2 gimbal up/down (the FPV's nose), R2 detonate/drop, hold ✕ back to the tank (recon: autopilot / take back), hold ○ artillery, △ thermal. **Hold to confirm:** ✕ and ○ act after 1 s (`HOLD_MS`); `#hold` shows a ring round the crosshair that fills by a CSS animation started when it is shown (no per-frame DOM writes) and a label from `G.droneLabel()` ('' = act at once: skipping the blast view, or nothing to launch). End card: ✕ restart, ○ continue after a victory. Snow, time of day, sound, music and debug are menu/keyboard only. The pad launch toast names pad controls (`ui.pad`); with a pad connected `body.pad` swaps the bottom hint bar `#keys` for `#padk` (tank) or `#padd` (drone). Keyboard and touch are unchanged (no hold). **Drone glide:** `glide()` in `combat.js` gives the velocity a firm rate while the sticks are deflected and a slow one when they are let go (FPV 2.6 / 1.3, bomber 3.2 / 1.4, recon 2 / 1.1 per s; height does not coast: released, it stops at 7/s), so it coasts like a DJI: about v / coast metres (FPV 20 m from 26 m/s, bomber 10 m from 14 m/s). **Drone view levels** (pad D-pad ↑↓ in the drone, mouse wheel, pinch; `D.view`, remembered per type in `G.dview`): FPV 0 chase far (7 m), 1 chase (2.3 m, default), 2 goggles (camera in the nose, bolted to the frame: it rolls with the sideways lean `-D.tr` plus a bank into pad yaw turns, and pitches only with the change of the forward lean `D.tp - D.tpS`, as if the camera were tilted up for cruise; bomber and recon cameras stay gimbal-stabilised); bomber/recon 0 outside (4.5 m behind, 1.8 m up, for landing on the tank), 1 gimbal camera (default), 2 and 3 optical zoom 2× / 4× (`R.dZoom` narrows the field and slows the look). `R.dFirst` hides the drone mesh. **Drone video:** the final pass (`post.wgsl`, `U.f`: x 1 analog goggles / 2 digital, y link quality) bends the FPV goggle picture (wide lens, dark rounded edges), washes out colour, adds scanlines, grain, torn lines and static as the link weakens; the digital (DJI-style) bomber/recon camera breaks into blocks below 55 % and darkens before it drops. Link quality `D.sig` (combat, every 0.25 s): 1 out to 70 % of the radio range, fading to 0 at the limit, × 0.4 with terrain between the tank and the drone (`radioPath`), × 0.75 through a building (smoke and trees do not matter). **OSD** (`#osd`, `U.osd` in `ui.js`, fed from `main.js` in first person only; writes at most every 0.15 s and only changed corners): analog FPV (RSSI, time, battery, km/h, height, home distance with arrow, LOW SIGNAL) and DJI-style (type and zoom, signal bars, battery, time, H, H.S, D with arrow, grenades, WEAK SIGNAL). Not checked on a phone layout yet. Pad yaw is a rate with momentum (`inp.yawR`, `D.yr` in `main.js`: 1.5 rad/s, spin-up 5/s, coast 4/s, about 21° after release); mouse turns stay direct. UI text is English in the code and English or Norwegian on screen (see **Language**).
- **Minimap (`minimap.js`):** the whole world is rendered once at load into a hidden canvas at the minimap's own scale (464 backing px for 2400 m, shown at 232 CSS px on desktop; touch 150 px, 104 or 96 px on a phone, where `drawMap` scales the markers up so they keep their size): relief shading, forest, water, bog, parking lots, dashed ski trails, roads (`W.roads`, wide ones brighter), buildings and the playable border. `drawMap` in `combat.js` (every 0.2 s) blits the window around the tank or drone at an integer offset and draws the markers on top. **Enemies appear only once seen** (`e.spotT` s left as a fresh contact, `e.seen`, last known `e.lx/lz`, `e.lostT`): the crew sees what is inside ±0.75 rad of the sight heading or within 180 m, on the enemy's own line-of-sight value (`e.los`, the same line reversed, > 0.2), out to 1600 m by day and less at night without the thermal sight (6 s); a muzzle flash within 2500 m (8 s); a hit (10 s); any drone's camera cone (±44°, `losVis` > 0.15, staggered every 0.3 s; FPV 800 m / 20 s, bomber 900 m / 25 s, recon 1500 m / 40 s). Fresh contacts are solid; a lost one is a hollow marker at the last known position that fades over 40 s (`LOST_T`); wrecks stay grey. The wave announcement still gives direction and range. Building damage repaints that building through `B.onState` (also on `restore`). **Large map (Tab, or a click/tap on the minimap; another closes it):** `G.bigMap()` builds a second, sharper copy of the base map on first use (`initMinimap(..., k)` with k ≤ 0.6 px/m; line widths scale with k; `B.onState` chains so both copies repaint damaged buildings) and draws the playable area plus 120 m into `#bmap` every 0.25 s with the same `markers()` as the minimap (500 m ring round the tank, scale bar); `markers(..., u)` scales every marker size and line width, u = CSS size / 380 clamped 1.2–2.6 on the big map, and large markers get a dark casing on the mine crosses. `tools/mapshot.mjs outdir [--touch --w --h]` stages every marker kind and saves the HUD, minimap, big map and a crop.
- **Language (`lang.js`):** English or Norwegian, chosen on the start screen (`#lLang`, top left; the owner asked for it there) or in the menu (row Language, `#mL`, `api.lang`; both through `pickLang` in `main.js`: `localStorage.glang`, applied by a reload), with `?lang=no|en`, else Norwegian for a Norwegian browser (`navigator.language` nb / nn / no). **English stays the source in all game code**; Norwegian is applied where text reaches the screen: `tr(s)` in `ui.js` (`toast`, `U.set` for the HUD pills and the objective pill, `U.card`, the hold label, the drone button, `CAMN`; `ui.tr` for the big map's label drawn in `combat.js`) and `trPage(document)` once at the top of `main.js` (text nodes of `#ui`, `#load`, `#err` by dictionary; the hint bars and both help grids are written whole from `HTML`). `tr` tries the dictionary `D` (which includes the mission texts `MSN`, keyed by the exact English in `missions.js`), then the patterns `R` (messages with numbers, names and compass directions; a pattern marked 1 may span a composite), then translates the parts of 'a · b' and of lines; what it does not know stays English. Military designations stay (APFSDS, HEAT-MP, HE, MG, FPV, ARTY, T-72B3), the drone OSD stays English, place names are untouched. Tests hook `ui.toast` before the translation and run in an en-US browser, so they see English. `node tools/langcheck.mjs [-v]` (no browser) fails on a mission text or a plain toast literal without a translation and on sample messages that come out unchanged or with English left in: run it after adding or rewording any message. **Message timing (`toast` in `ui.js`):** a message stays 2.5 s plus 55 ms per character (at most 9 s; 1.4 s for everything before); a new one waits until the one on screen has had 1.5 s (a queue of 3, the oldest dropped), except a warning (an exclamation mark in the English), which is shown at once.
- **`tod.js`:** four time-of-day presets (T cycles them, or the menu), interpolated into the global uniforms.
