# Sound, music and vibration

`audio.js` (recorded loops and procedural sound), `music.js` and the title screen, controller vibration (`haptics.js`). Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **Sound (`audio.js`, `sndrec.js`):** the engines, tracks and drone are recorded loops; everything else is procedural Web Audio. Recorded: six short MP3s (24 kHz mono, 40 kbit/s, about 84 kB, about 110 kB in the bundle) in `src/data/snd/`, cut by `tools/snd/prep.py <source dir>` from Freesound files (sources and authors in `tools/snd/CREDITS.md`; the originals are not in the repo). Each file holds a 0.1 s margin, the loop body L and F seconds of continuation (`meta.json`); `decode()` builds the seamless loop after `decodeAudioData` by cross-fading the start with the continuation, so the seam does not depend on the browser's MP3 decoder delay. `sndrec.js` holds the imports so `audio.js` stays importable in Node. Player engine: three layers at one pitch (`e0` Leopard 2A4 idle, `e1` Leopard running, `e2` a heavier engine with its whine notched out), equal-power cross-faded by load (`ld` from throttle and speed), playback rate 0.85 at idle to about 1.45; tracks `tk` at a rate following speed; enemies a T-26 on the move (`en`, engine and tracks together); drone `dr`, a fly-by resampled to a constant rotor pitch, played twice detuned 2.7 %. Procedural: `synth()` renders 13 mono buffers at load (pure JS, about 0.15 s; `node tools/audio-check.mjs` prints peak/RMS per buffer and fails on NaN or clipping): guns, explosions, hits, reload, smoke, collapse, crash, MG loop and a noise buffer for the snow crunch; the turret whine is two oscillators. The `AudioContext` is created on the first gesture; without one the game runs silent. The tank's voices sum into one bus that is attenuated, low-passed and panned from the camera like any source (so it fades in the drone view); three enemy voices go to the nearest live enemies. One-shots arrive through `C.sfx(name, x, y, z, k)` (`gun egun auto boom hit reload smoke collapse crash whistle atgm mgb incoming`) and play on a pool of 12 chains (lowpass, gain, stereo pan; the quietest is dropped when full) with distance gain, air-absorption lowpass and a `d / 340` s delay (cap 3 s); cannons beyond 300 m use the distant-report buffer. `update(dt)` only sets AudioParams and runs before the `simOnly` return. Master 0.6 through a compressor; M or the menu toggles it (`localStorage.gsnd`), muting also suspends the context. **Sound is on by default**; a stored choice (`localStorage.gsnd`) and `?mute` win. On iOS audio only unlocks on the release of a touch, needs `navigator.audioSession.type = 'playback'` to ignore the silent switch, and can be `interrupted`; that fix is untested on a device.
- **Music (`music.js`) and title screen:** the whole of the owner's "Norwegian Tank Battle" (`src/data/music.mp3`, 200 s, 48 kbit/s joint stereo, looped) in an `HTMLAudioElement` on a blob URL (playback stays off the main thread while the world is generated; the element is built on first need). It tries to play at once (works where the browser allows autoplay) and otherwise starts on the first click or key, so picking a world starts it; it plays through loading and into the game, where it glides from volume 0.6 to 0.3 (`music.game()`) under the game sounds. Toggles: the MUSIC button on the start screen (`#lMu`) and in the top bar (`#bMu`, so touch has it), the menu row (`#mU`) and U, all through `localStorage.gmus`; a press while it is on but blocked or not started means "play", not "off". The sound switch (M / menu) silences it too (`music.sound`). It does not start on its own in scripted runs (`nohint auto perf`). iOS ignores `volume`, so there it plays at full level in the game. The start screen (`#load`) shows `src/data/title.webp` (the owner's 1672×941 artwork, WebP quality 70, 237 kB) with a dark gradient, set as a data URL from `main.js` at load; title and world buttons sit at the bottom over it.
- **Controller vibration (`haptics.js`):** `initHaptics({ veh, game, ui, level })` (`R.hap`, `window.__hap`). The Gamepad API's `dual-rumble` (strong = low-frequency motor, weak = high-frequency; Firefox falls back to `hapticActuators[0].pulse`). Every event that goes through the `sfx` wrapper in `main.js` also reaches `R.hap.ev(name, x, y, z, k)`, with distance from the tank (or the drone while flying it): own gun, hits on the tank (by damage), explosions (by size and distance: artillery is felt 100 m off, a small blast at 400 m not at all), collapses, nearby enemy cannon, felled trees (`crash` with k < 1) and walls, smoke launch, breech/repair clank, and `land` (k = impact speed after a crest; sfx-only event that audio ignores). `update(dt)` (after `audio.update`, also in `__sim`) adds a continuous layer: the engine as pulses on the strong motor that follow the engine sound's rpm (3 Hz deep chug at idle to 7 Hz smoother throb at full revs, plus a little of the weak motor at high rpm, all scaled by `ENG` = 0.65: peak about 0.023 at idle and 0.065 at full revs; the owner found the first steady version too strong, then asked for a bit less again), tracks by speed on the weak one (softer on roads), turret traverse, an MG rattle that alternates per effect, `veh.shake`. Flying a drone (not the recon autopilot) drops the tank layer: the FPV hums faintly on the weak motor with its speed (0.025–0.065), bomber and recon are still apart from events (a grenade release ticks). Kicks are a pool of 8 linear fades. The mix is re-sent about every 30–45 ms (faster with the engine pulse rate, about four samples per pulse; the interval accumulates so the average holds at 60 fps; 120 ms effects, so they overlap); a reused parameter object, the promise is the browser's. Stops on a hidden page, level off or no pad; with the help or menu open only kicks run. `ui.js` exposes the pad it polled (`U.pad`) and `U.modalOpen`. Menu row Vibration Off / Light (×0.55) / Full (`#mV`, `localStorage.gvib`, `?novib`); choosing a level gives a short test buzz. Nothing can feel it here: magnitudes are untuned guesses until the owner has tried them on the DualSense.
