# Drones and enemy air

The player's FPV, bomber and recon drones (flight, cameras, video look, OSD is under ui.md), enemy FPV, Lancet and Orlan, the hatch MG, cope cages. Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **Drones (X launches, Z / TYPE / L3 picks the type; `DTYPE` in `combat.js`, stock `G.dn`, selection `G.dsel`, `D.type`):** FPV (5), bomber `BOMB` (2) and recon `RECON` (2). Battery times are compressed for the 3 km map (real ones run 10–40 min): FPV 2 min, bomber 4, recon 6 (raised from 75/120/180 s). The HUD pill reads `FPV 5` on the tank and `BOMB ×3 · 95 s` in flight; the touch DRONE button names the selected type and the in-flight action button reads BOOM, DROP or is hidden (recon). Bomber and recon fly level on the heading (WASD), hold their height (Space/Shift; they climb to 30 m / 110 m after launch until you touch the height), and the camera is a gimbal under the airframe (`main.js`, drone mesh not drawn in its own view) that tilts down to vertical. **Bomber:** 14 m/s, 240 s, 3 VOG grenades per sortie (LMB, 0.7 s apart): shell type 7 falls with the normal shell ballistics; `predictDrop` marches the same parabola (ground, vehicles, roofs, refined by bisection) and the impact point is drawn as the gun-marker ring (`R.dropSx/Sy`, green when a grenade is ready). Damage from above: T-72 turret roof 50, engine deck (hull, local z > 0.9) 85 and immobilised, elsewhere 30; BMP 110; buildings 25; small splash. Landing on the tank (within 4.5 m, below hull + 4.5 m, under 5 m/s, after it has been 15 m away) puts the drone back in stock with fresh grenades. **Recon:** 18 m/s, 360 s, 2200 m radio, quiet (rotor gain 0.14 vs 0.4), unarmed; it spots for the map and can land back too. Crashes lose the bomber or recon drone without a warhead blast. **Recon on watch:** X with the recon up does not cut the link: the drone moves to its own slot (`RW` = `G.watch`, drawn with a second drone object `droneO2`; `updateWatch`) and an autopilot circles it 80 m round the spot where it was left at its height, the camera sweeping round and down (`droneScan(T, S)` takes the drone that looks), until the battery is flat or it is out of radio range; you are back in the tank (the main gun works). **The flown-drone slot `D` is then free:** Z picks another type and X launches it, so an FPV or the bomber can go out while the recon spots (the owner asked for this); with RECON picked, X takes the watching drone back instead of launching a second one (it counts down on the pill as `RECON↑ n s`, and both drones are on the map). `D.auto` is no longer set; one recon can watch at a time. **FPV:** `updateDrone` flies it with the free-fly inputs (mouse turns it, WASD, Space/Shift up/down, 26 m/s, 120 s battery, 1500 m radio range) while the camera sits rigidly just behind it (a lagging follow jitters at that speed when the frame time varies) and the minimap centres on it. After a detonation the camera pulls back and watches the blast for 4.5 s (`D.hold`; LMB or X skips) before returning to the tank. LMB, a crash, a building or touching a vehicle detonates the shaped charge (kills a T-72 within about 3 m); X again cuts the link and the drone is lost. The tank stands still and can be hit meanwhile; enemies do not shoot at the drone. The model and `droneParts` (airframe plus four rotor parts) are in `tank.js`.
- **Enemy air (`combat.js`, phase 3):** a pool of `MAXA` = 6 (`G.air`; types in `ATYPE`: 0 FPV, 1 Lancet, 2 Orlan-10), each with its own uniform block (`aObj` in `main.js`) and one of three meshes (`buildDrone`, `buildLancet`, `buildOrlan` in `tank.js`; `airParts` turns the propeller about the long axis). `planAir` (at a wave start) schedules raids on the main axis: an FPV from wave 2, an Orlan from wave 3, a second FPV and a Lancet from wave 4, more in wave 6; `airSpawn(type, bearing)` puts one 1300 m out. **FPV / Lancet:** transit at 28 / 110 m above ground to a rough fix on the tank, then search in a 70 m circle; every 0.4 s within `see` (380 / 750 m) a lock chance `pc × line of sight × (1 − 0.75 canopy) × (1 − 0.5 speed/8) × smoke × range` (smoke near the tank: Lancet 0, FPV × 0.3); a glimpse narrows the search. Locked, they dive with lead (38 / 68 m/s) and hit for 25–40 / 40–55 hull; a miss blows up on the ground (splash, sets off mines). Smoke within 35 m of the tank breaks a dive (Lancet fast, FPV rarely). Battery 160 / 220 s. Toasts at `warn` range (650 / 420 m) and on the dive; the HUD draws red chevrons for the two most pressing (blinking while diving) in `post.wgsl` from `G.thr` (the final-pass uniform grew a fifth vec4, `e`, and later a sixth, `f`, for the drone video look; all post buffers are now 96 bytes). **Orlan:** circles 280 m round a lagging fix on the tank at 180 m, and while it sees the tank (`G.orlan` counts seconds; phase 5 artillery will use it) every enemy `known` ≥ 4. Shot down it falls trailing smoke. **Hits:** the player's rounds (types 0–3) test each step's segment against the air units (`airHit`; radius `ATYPE.r`); an MG round is one hit point (FPV 1, Lancet 2, Orlan 4). Air contacts that have been announced show as red carets on the minimap. Sound: the nearest air unit gets the drone recording, pitched up for FPV / Lancet and down for the Orlan. **One MG control (Space, touch MG, pad ✕):** the coax by default; it switches to the hatch MG3 when the commander's view or the gunner sight is pitched above 0.3 rad (higher than the gun elevates) or an air unit is within ~5° of the crosshair (`G.hmgAuto`); Q and pad R3 force the hatch MG (there is no separate touch button any more). **Hatch MG3:** 20 rounds/s from the commander's mount on the shared 7.62 belt; it fires along the view and converges on the range of an air unit within ±0.05 rad of the sight line (so leading still ranges), else on the ground or building on the sight line, else at 400 m; the commander's view pitch limit is now 1.2 rad (70°; the gunner sight keeps 0.75). **Cope cage:** from wave 3, 40 % of T-72s (`e.cage`, mesh `buildT72(true)`, `eMesh[2]`): the player's FPV charge does 35 % and a bomber grenade on the roof or engine deck 30 % (and no immobilisation).
