# The world

`world.js`, roads and parking, props, destructible buildings, lidar in the game (ski jumps, arena, landmarks), Holmenkollbanen, the fantasy world, the playable area. Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **`world.js`:** the real world. It inflates `src/data/world.bin` (lidar height grid at `meta.hstep` = 4 m, 2 m detail insets `ins<i>` / `meta.insets`, water / wetland rasters, lidar canopy cover `cov` (4 m) and canopy height `canh` (16 m), trail polylines, building rectangles `bld` with lidar roof data `bldh`, road polylines, tunnel segments, parking polygons), upsamples the heights bicubically to the 2 m heightmap, adds only a fifth of the old noise when the grid is lidar (`NZ`), writes the insets over it (feathered over 16 m), flattens lakes, carves trails, levels building pads, and plants trees steered by the density raster. Landmark positions are in `LM`.
- **Roads and parking (real world only):** the whole OSM road network of the window is plowed (`tools/geo/fetch_roads.py` → `raw/osm_roads.json`, `roads.py` rewrites blob `roads` and `meta.roads` = [n, width×10, class], `meta.rbr` bridges, `meta.rtun` other tunnels; class 0 = the seven original roads, kept byte for byte, 1–3 secondary/tertiary/unclassified, 4 residential, 5 living street, 6 service, 7 driveway ≥ 30 m / parking aisle; not plowed: footways, paths, tracks, gravel or closed forest roads (`snowplowing` tag wins), unpaved service roads, Kongeveien's 81 m tunnel under the landing hill). `plowRoads` (module level) profiles every road from the ground before the house pads (`H0`), pins a road to the level of one laid before where it joins it (blended over 30 m), lays tagged bridges straight across at grade, gives minor roads (class ≥ 4) only the asphalt plus a 2.5 m shoulder and never touches house footprints + 1 m (`footMask`); trees keep their crown off the asphalt. `roadNet` (enemy routing) holds classes 0–3 and streets over 250 m. The minimap draws classes ≥ 4 thin and dim. `world.js` levels each parking lot to a least-squares plane and gives each road a flat bed along a smoothed profile, then writes the distance to the asphalt edge into `mat` G (0.667 = edge, 6 m ramp; lots are capped at 0.8 so the shader can tell them apart). `terrain.wgsl` turns that into asphalt, packed snow and snowbanks when `ROADS` is 1.0 (`main.js` patches the constant; in the fantasy world G is still creek water). Holmenkollveien's two tunnels under the ski bridges at the arena (`meta.tunnels`) are functional bridges now (see **Bridges**): `plowRoads` lays the road straight through at grade between the levels outside them (no sinking, no `portal` headwalls); the old tunnel handling (`cov`, `portals`, `portal()` in `props.js`) remains for tunnels without a `meta.bridges` record. Enemies route through them too (the `roadNet` cut round converted tunnels was removed in milestone 2).
- **`props.js`:** static set pieces in one mesh. Real world: houses from building rectangles, the Frognerseteren lodge, ski jumps, Tryvannstårnet, the chapel. Fantasy world: cabin, bridge, dam, pylons, signs. Houses, the lodge and the chapel push a record into `W.bld` (vertex range `v0..v1` in the props mesh, colour, footprint area and one or more solid boxes: centre, builder yaw, half-extents, pad height, wall height, gable rise); everything else (lamps, pylons, boulders, the TV tower, ski-jump supports and the low end of the inruns, tunnel headwalls, the fantasy cabin) stays a circle in `W.colliders`.
- **Buildings (`buildings.js`, real world):** `initBuildings(W, propsMesh, HALF)` turns `W.bld` into typed arrays (`x y z hp hpM st v0 v1 b0 b1 size`, boxes in `box` with stride 10) and a 16 m grid in which each box is listed in every cell within 4.5 m of it, so a point or vehicle-sized circle needs one cell. Queries (no allocation): `at(x,y,z,pad)` (box and roof profile, used by shells and the drone), `push(x,z,r,out)` (circle push-out along the smallest penetration), `ray`/`seg` (sight ray ranging and line of sight; rubble does not block), `rubble(x,z)` (slows vehicles to about half speed), `splash` (HE 30 at the wall falling to 0 at 9 m; drone 30 within 5 m). Damage: direct HE 70, HEAT-MP 45, APFSDS 20, enemy 125 mm 35, 30 mm 3, MG 0, drone 100; `hpM` = 60 + 0.5 × footprint m², clamped 60–400. States 0 intact, 1 damaged (< 50 %), 2 rubble. A change copies the building's pristine range once (`pris`), rewrites it in the CPU copy of the props vertices and uploads only that range (`B.upload`, a `writeBuffer` of `v0*56` bytes in); damaged = sagging ridge and corner, half the windows dark, walls and roof switched to `MAT.charred` (soot and burnt-through patches in `object.wgsl` that glow at night); rubble = the shell sinks below ground and `debris()` writes a heap of tilted boxes (`MAT.rubble`: a mound of blocks over the footprint, beams, slabs, roof sheets, wall stumps and corner posts) into a separate ring buffer of `DMAX` = 6144 boxes (`debO` in `main.js`, drawn after the props in the main and shadow passes). `restore()` (from `G.restart`) puts every changed range back, resets hp and state and empties the debris ring. Player: `collide(dt)` in `main.js` pushes three hull circles (r 1.9 m at −1.8/0/+1.8 m) out of the boxes, loses the speed into the wall on impact, grinds along it afterwards, and tests circle colliders from one grid cell; 10 000 calls take about 2–4 ms (about 150 ms before, when it looped over all 5847 circles).
- **Lidar in the game:** trees are planted where the lidar has canopy (chance from `cov`, `TK_IN` 0.5 in the playable area: 0.58 gave denser forest and twice the stuck events in `wavetest`), with heights from `canh`, not inside house footprints or ski jumps (`inJump`). Houses use the measured eave, rise, flat or pitched roof and ridge direction (`B.h` in `house()`). At tunnels the lidar already has the road at its level and the earth on top, so nothing is sunk (`LID` in the roads block). **Ski jumps** (`skiJump` in `props.js`) are built from profiles in `LM` (`world.js`): positions (d, t) = metres from the top along the axis and to the side, absolute heights; `deck` is the inrun, `plat` the flat top, `wing` Holmenkollbakken's wind screens beside the landing hill, `block` the building under the take-off, `skirt` an inrun standing on the ground (Midtstubakken). The landing hills are terrain. Holmenkollbakken follows the architect's section for its side profile (the owner supplied section, plan and photos): the track is analytic (`LM.jump.track`: straight at 36° from d 18 / 420.5 m, one arc of radius about 112 m, a 6.7 m table at 11°, lip at d 106 / 373 m, 3 m above the knoll; straight length and radius are solved in `skiJump` from the endpoints), carried by an open trussed wedge with a straight bottom chord from under the tip to a compact dark base at the knoll (d 86), a pair of raking struts, air under the cantilever, a thin level roof plate at 423.5 m and no platform box. The clad silver band above the track is the screen (2.65 m at the start growing to 7.5 m at the lip) and continues without a kink as the wind-screen wings: a clad band 8 m deep tapering to 1.5 m at the tip, its top a cubic through the lidar wing tops, leaning in towards the hill, on open framing where it stands clear of the ground. Stairs, ice grooves, light strips, banner boards along the hill edges (`edge`) and `hut`s (judges' tower, dark block, visitor centre). The lidar 'deck' measured for the first version was the top of the mesh screens, not the track: the surface model returns the highest thing, so read tracks and floors from drawings or the ground under them. `hb` is the inrun half width. **Arena (`arena()` in `props.js`, tables in `LM.arena`):** stands as ribs `[d, t front, t back]` in a frame (a jump's axis or two points), laid as stepped rows on the terrain (`stand`; with `x: 1` the rows run across the slope instead, as contour bands of the plane fitted to the ground, `standX`: the terraces beside Holmenkollbakken's landing hill, at right angles to the bowl's rows): the amphitheatre round the outrun (`bowl()`, ring entries with `o` top-of-rows line, `i` foot line, `w` foot of the outrun wall, traced from the architect's plan and the lidar: level contour rows `top - k * riser`, about 0.87 m per 2 m row, radial aisles every 24 m of rim, a rim walkway, an oblique upper edge meeting the wedge stands with a 5 m walkway between, a north ring and on the south an outer ring over a narrow steep band; 4766 triangles), the stands beside the landing hill, the 168 m main cross-country / biathlon stand with its commentator building, the biathlon range (30 lanes), Midtstubakken's judges' tower and stands, a finish gantry (position guessed) and 12 floodlight masts (positions from OSM, heights from lidar; they light the snow at night through `nrm` A and get glow sprites via `W.glows`). 9714 triangles, 271 collision circles (`W.arenaStat`); the stands are not destructible. `inJump` / `inArena` (module level in `world.js`, behind the `inAB` bounding box) keep trees and OSM houses out. **Landmarks rebuilt from lidar sizes with Wikimedia Commons photos as an eye reference:** Tryvannstårnet (ribbed drum, glass band, equipment drum with dishes, grey lattice mast to 104 m and whip to 118 m; 2878 triangles), Holmenkollen kapell (T-plan, crossing tower with spire at the east end, aisles, porch; 9 collision boxes), Frognerseteren (now facing south over the city with a stone terrace, cross gable, east wing and a separate annex record; the start point is pinned so it did not move). A chairlift (`skiLift`, `LM.lift`) runs north-west from the tower. `edgeBlur` smooths height, normals and canopy along the map border, because the ground beyond the window repeats the edge texels (streaks to the horizon otherwise). Not modelled: stadium service buildings, the small K10–K60 jumps, see-through mesh on the wind screens.
- **Holmenkollbanen (T-bane line 1):** `tools/geo/fetch_rail.py` (Overpass: rail features plus roads, paths and pistes, cached in `raw/osm_rail.json`) and `rail.py [--overlay] [--dry]` (blob `rail`: per track ~2 m points x, z, ballast-top height, bridge flag; meta `rail`: stations with platform rectangles at lidar height, buffer stops, crossings with kind and clearance, parked trains). OSM sits within 0.1 m of the lidar bed (no shift applied). 3 tracks, 14.6 km in the window, 20 platforms at 10 stations (Frognerseteren to Vettakollen). `railWorld` in `world.js` levels the bed 0.3 m under the ballast top (the terrain shader adds up to ~0.4 m soft snow), lowers the ground under platforms, keeps trees and boulders off (`railMask`) and drops OSM outlines within 2.2 m of a track (`B.gone`; shelters there are rebuilt by the station props). `railway()` in `props.js`: ballast strips with `MAT.ballast` 29 (sleepers and snow in `object.wgsl`), rails and a covered third rail between the tracks, open over bridge spans; platforms, shelters (timber at Frognerseteren and Holmenkollen), lamps (`W.railGlow`, light on the snow via `spill`), name boards, T signs, buffer stops, MX3000 units at Frognerseteren and Holmenkollen. Platforms, trains and buffer stops are `W.bld` records with `fix: 1` (solid, not destructible, not on the minimap's house layer); rails have no colliders, so the tank crosses anywhere at grade. Bridges and underpasses are not built (listed in `meta.rail.x`); the line has a gap there and the road passes under at its own level. About 60k props triangles (track ~46k).
- **`fantasy.js`:** the original procedural world generator. Both generators return the same `W` shape (including `W.PLAY`, `W.MW`, `W.TREELINE`, `W.name`, `W.startI`), and `main.js` / `props.js` branch on `W.name` where they differ.

## Gotchas
- Lake material (`mat` R) also blocks tree placement (`> 8`), so terrain edits on lakes have to keep R ≥ 9.
- Building vertices are edited in place in the CPU copy of the props mesh: always restore the pristine range before applying a state, and keep any per-vertex noise a function of position only, or shared box corners of different faces move apart and crack. Rubble built by squashing the original mesh read as a flat plate (a wall box or roof quad has only its corner vertices to move), hence the separate debris boxes.
- Lidar gotchas: a cable along the axis of Holmenkollbakken reads as 10–30 m of 'structure' on the centre line of the landing hill (measure the hill off-axis or from the DTM); under a removed bridge deck (the two ski bridges over Holmenkollveien, the road and rail bridges) the DTM holds a smooth interpolated fill across the hole, neither the road below nor earth: take the lower level from a grade fitted outside both ends, and the deck from the DOM (`tools/geo/bridges.py`); OSM outlines of stands and jump parts arrive as building rectangles (flag 8 in `bldh`). Subagents working in the same checkout can leave the build broken for a minute: a page that will not load right after a build may be someone else's half-made edit.
- `genWorld` in `world.js` is close to the size where V8 stops optimising a function: after the arena and border code were added inline, every loop in it ran about twice as slow and loading went from 4 to 8–12 s, with no single hot line in the profile. Keep new world-generation steps in module-level functions (`edgeBlur`, `inJump`, `inArena`) and check the load time (`ready` in about 4.5 s on the Linux machine) after adding to it.
- Building rectangles: `house()` draws with `at(-B.yaw)`, so a building's local x axis is (cos yaw, sin yaw) in world x, z, and world -> building is `u = dx cos + dz sin`, `v = -dx sin + dz cos`. `tools/geo/buildings.py` had it right; `compare.py`, `canopy.py` and the tree / road / rail masks in `world.js` had the mirrored angle (found in review: 191 tree trunks inside rotated houses, 0 after). Check against the lidar when in doubt: for elongated rotated buildings the right convention covers 0.86 of the roof, the mirrored one 0.65.
- **Playable area:** a box, not a square, in the real world: `W.PB` = [-1850, -1560, 1560, 1750] (x0, z0, x1, z1), with `W.inP(x, z, margin)`, `W.cpx`, `W.cpz` (from `playBox` in `math.js`; the fantasy world gets its square). It was widened west and south so the whole metro line Holmenkollen – Frognerseteren (Skogen at x -1815, Holmenkollen station at z 1670) is inside; the window is still ±2048, so only about 200 m (west) and 300 m (south) remain to the edge there, against 490 m north and east. `W.PLAY` (1560) is kept as the old square for the tests that stage on the lake. The snow mask `MW` went from 3200 to 3712 m (0.91 m per texel instead of 0.78) to cover the box. The big map is centred on the box. Beyond the window the real world's ground is rolling hills shaded as snow-laden forest (`terrain.wgsl`, only where `ROADS` is 1 and outside ±`G.world.x`), because the bare white plain was plainly visible from the new west border. The alpine slope north of the tower lies partly outside the window and needs a larger world.
