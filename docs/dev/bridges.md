# Bridges

Functional two-level crossings: data, world carving, queries, and what every moving thing must do near them. Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **Bridges (`src/bridges.js`, `tools/geo/bridges.py`):** crossings where both levels work. The heightfield stays the LOWER level; each deck is an overlay slab and the walls are oriented boxes that block only at their own height; nothing goes into `W.colliders` / `W.bld`. **Crossings** come from the data (`bridges.py`, lidar pass, writes `meta.bridges`, prints a table): `meta.tunnels` (k 0: Holmenkollveien under the two arena ski bridges), `meta.rail.x` kind 1 (k 1: the metro over a road or path; the deck carries the track) and kind 2 with `meta.rbr` (k 2: a road or track over the metro; the lower level is the rail bed), plus the hand table `CORR` (skips, names, the Heftyebakken cavity: crowns over a forest track spoil the surface model, so its deck is the line between the approach levels). Kind 1 is built inside the playable area, kind 2 also a little outside; crossings with skew over ~25° are skipped (Holmenkollveien at z 1976, 51°). Per crossing: the frame is aligned with the over-route (k 0: with the road), u along it, v across; the lower level along v: the DTM grade fitted 1.5–10 m outside the measured deck faces (roads sag under bridges; under a removed deck the DTM holds a fill) or, for k 2, the ballast profile − 0.3 m (the bed `railWorld` levels); the deck extent along v (DOM > 3 m over the lower level), the cavity along u (DOM over the DTM fill), the trench (what runs under ± 4 m, widened by the skew, clipped to the cavity), the slab (the cavity + 1.5 m and at least 7 m past the trench), the deck plane (robust least squares on the DOM; k 1: 5 cm under the ballast top, level across), slab thickness per kind (`TH` 1.1 / 1.3 (0.9 m concrete + 0.4 m packed snow round the ballast) / 1.0 m), clearance over the under-route's centre line at both faces. Under 3.4 m a crossing is `blk`: built with a height barrier (`WK` 4, push only: a striped beam on posts in front of each face) when the clearance is at least 1.8 m, otherwise not built at all (`BUILT` in world.js: the metro runs on through on an embankment, as at the path at (456, 1446)). Record: `name k src c u hl hw y0 gu gv th tr r0 rg clr [blk] [rv rw] [tun]`, deck top `y0 + gu lu + gv lv`, v = (-uz, ux); `rv`/`rw`: where the road runs on a k 2 deck. The table at the end of milestone 2: see `bridges.py`'s output (10 built: 2 ski bridges, Øvreseterveien and Lillevannsveien under the metro, Tomm Murstadbakken, Heftyebakken and Lillevannsveien (z 1712, outside the playable area) over it, 3 blocked: Frognerseterveien (2.5 m, on the edge of the playable area), the track at (-1357, -710) (2.5 m), the path at (-1132, 1003) (2.0 m)). **World** (`world.js`, module level): `bridgeWorld` carves the trench at the lower level (and 3.2 m on behind the abutments inside the faces, so the step up hides under the slab), puts the ground under the rest of the slab 0.3 m below its bottom (the last 3 m at each slab end at the deck plane, so a hull corner never dips), ramps the earth beyond the slab ends fully to the deck plane for 3 m, fading out by 10 m (13 m sideways: a steeper fade read as rock beside the faces), clears the asphalt (mat G) on the rail bed under a k 2 deck; `plowRoads` lays the road straight through at grade under k 0 decks (no sinking, no `portal`; the old tunnel handling remains for tunnels without a record) and leaves the stretch on a k 2 deck alone (the asphalt there is a prop); `railWorld` keeps the lidar ground only at the spans of built k 1 decks and levels the bed through every other span; trees, trail lamps and boulders keep off (`brTex`, `railMask`). **Queries** (`initBridges(list, heightAt)`, main.js, before the props; `W.br`, `W.groundAt`; the fantasy world gets an empty set): `DK` (stride 10) and `WL` (stride 9: centre, axis, half-length, half-thickness, bottom, top, slope; `WK` 0 abutment from below the road to the slab bottom, 1 portal face, 2 parapet from top − 0.2 to top + 1.1, 3 wing wall falling from the slab to the ground beside the trench mouth, 4 height barrier), per-bridge and global AABBs, and (no allocation) `top(x, z, y)` (a querier at y ≥ top − `STEP` (1 m) stands on the deck), `groundAt(x, z, y)` = max(heightAt, top), `over` (lowest slab bottom above y), `inSolid`, `seg` (exact entry fraction into a slab or wall: the segment sheared into the slab's frame; barriers excluded; 2 if none), `push(x, z, y, r, out)` (walls overlapping [y + 0.3, y + 2.5]), `id(x, z, pad)`, `shade` (canopy density 110 in nrm B over the trench, written for the GPU upload only and undone after it, so line of sight and enemy routing do not see the underpass as forest). **Player:** track corners `groundAt(x, z, veh.y)`, airborne when the ground falls more than 1.2 m under the hull, a step up of more than 0.2 m in a frame is climbed, not launched from; `collide` pushes the hull circles out of the walls; no track stamps on a deck (`veh.deck`); the commander camera stays below the slab under a deck (pulled in towards the turret if the ground or a wall is in the way), drone cameras the same; `__tp(x, z, yaw, top)` puts the tank on the highest surface. **Weapons and sensors:** one `seg` per shell per frame in `updateShells`, `G.aimHit` and the drone artillery aim clipped by `seg`, `explode` treats a deck as ground but stamps no scorch there, `losVis` and `radioPath` blocked by slabs and walls, drones use `inSolid` and `groundAt` (an FPV flies under a deck and crashes into its side), no drone launch under a deck. **Enemies (milestone 2):** corner sampling `groundAt(x, z, e.y)`; `C.solid(x, z, r, out, y)` adds the bridge walls (with the contact normal, so they slide along); separation, column keeping and the push from the player ignore vehicles more than 3 m apart in height, but only within 3 m of a bridge (applied everywhere, two vehicles side by side on a steep slope stopped separating: wheeled vehicles drove through forest 4–7 % of the time in `roadtest` instead of 1–2 %); spawn (8 m) and `okSpot` (10 m) reject bridge footprints, `findRoute` takes no join point within 6 m of a bridge (the trail over a deck is never 'nearest' to a vehicle in the road under it); the `roadNet` cut round converted tunnels is gone, so road-bound vehicles drive through under both arena bridges and a vehicle on trail 14 crosses the deck. **Mines:** none buried on or under a bridge (`layField`), surface mines land on a deck (`groundAt`), the pressure fuze needs the tank within 2.5 m in height (and not 0.8 m above it), clearing by an impact needs the mine within 3 m in height. **Infantry:** `footOk` rejects footprints (3 m), movement `groundAt` at the soldier's own height and `C.solid` with y, dismounts land on a deck if the vehicle stands on one, the tracks kill only within 1.5 m in height; `soldier.js` gets `W.groundAt` and passes the soldier's height for the foot IK and the lying tilt. **Particles:** chunks (kind 0) land on a deck (`parts.update` gets `W.groundAt`), smoke clouds settle on it. **Props:** `overpass()` draws exactly the collision boxes (`obox`, sheared): dark soffit with ribs, fascia beams, the deck surface per kind (snow; packed snow round the metro's ballast; a plowed road strip with snow beside it), a snow wedge at each slab end that runs down into the ramp's snow, concrete walls (`MAT.concrete` 30: formwork joints, rain streaks; exempt from the commander view's sight fade), snow caps, tunnel lights on the abutments, the height barriers; `railway()` draws the track continuous over every span. Minimap: the deck pale over the under-route, the over-route redrawn, parapet lines with ticks. Not done: Kongeveien's 81 m tunnel under the landing hill (needs a tunnel type: an overlay ceiling heightfield rather than a plane slab), crossings with a large skew, enemy AI that plans across levels on purpose (it simply follows the routes). Tests: `node --expose-gc tools/bridgecheck.mjs` (no GPU) and `tools/bridgetest.mjs [outdir] [--shotsonly] [--noshots]` (renders in `shots/bridges/`).

## Gotchas
- Anything that moves must test bridge slabs with `W.br.seg` on its movement segment before using `groundAt`: `groundAt` accepts a querier up to `STEP` below a deck top, so a slow riser under a deck would otherwise be lifted onto it (player drone), and a fast diver passes through (enemy FPV / Lancet).
