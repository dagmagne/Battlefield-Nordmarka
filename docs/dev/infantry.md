# Infantry

Soldier model, poses and rendering (`soldier.js`), infantry logic (`infantry.js`): RPG and ATGM teams, dismounts. Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **Infantry rendering (`soldier.js`, `soldier.wgsl`, `soldiershadow.wgsl`; phase 6A):** the interface with the infantry logic is `G.inf = { n, d }` (created in `initCombat`, `MAXI` = 40): `n` = slots in use (upper bound for iteration), `d` = `Float32Array(MAXI * 8)`, per soldier `[x, y, z, yaw, pose, phase, aim, state]`. `x y z` = feet on the ground, `yaw` as for vehicles (forward = (−sin yaw, 0, −cos yaw)); `pose` 0 stand, 1 walk, 2 run, 3 kneel-aim, 4 prone-aim, 5 stand-aim, 6 dead, 7 RPG-aim (kneeling, RPG-7 on the shoulder, rifle slung; every other pose carries the AK-74); `phase` = animation clock in seconds (dead: seconds since death); `aim` = weapon pitch (rad, up positive); `state` 0 unused, 1 alive, 2 dead (state 2 means dead whatever the pose). The walk/run cycles match `WALK_V` 1.45 m/s (`WALK_HZ` 0.92) and `RUN_V` 3.9 m/s (`RUN_HZ` 1.38): advance `phase` by `dt × speed / WALK_V` (or `RUN_V`) and the planted foot does not slide (measured 0.02 / 0.06 m/s). The soldier is 13 rigid parts built with `MB` (`buildSoldier(lo)`: pelvis, torso with a grey chest rig, pouches, a rounded pack with straps and the hood, head with face, gaiter and a cloth helmet cover with a brim, upper arms, forearms with olive gloves, thighs, shins with grey-brown boots (rounded toe), a 0.88 m AK-74 with dark wood furniture, RPG-7), about 1.82 m; limbs are lofts with dome ends centred on their joints, so bent joints do not open gaps. Near mesh 2032 triangles (1804 without the RPG, whose matrix is zero in other poses), far LOD (beyond 120 m) 244, posed by the same matrices. Materials in `object.wgsl` (pattern fixed to the part): `MAT.cloth` 24 (overall: creases, weave, worn patches, seams, dirt within 0.5 m of the ground), `skin` 25, `gear` 26 (rig, straps), `pack` 27 (pack, boots), `glove` 28; albedo clamped to 0.74 so sunlit suits stay under the snow. The instance's extra vec4 is (heat, tint seed, warmth seed, 0): `soldier.wgsl` varies each soldier's shade and warmth by ±5 % (not the weapons). Thermal, scaled by heat (`aux` varying, 1 alive, falls to 0.15 over 60 s after death): face 1.05, gloves 0.95, overall 0.78 (0.58 low on the legs), rig 0.66, pack and boots 0.4; weapons use the default 0.24. `initSoldiers(MAXI, heightAt)` returns `SOL`; `SOL.update(inf, dt, cam, planes, n)` (every rendered frame in `main.js`) tracks pose changes per slot (0.25 s cross-fade between poses; on death a staged fall from the last alive pose: a slump key (knees buckle, then hips and torso, arms go slack) over 0.3 s, then the topple about the feet onto the back or front, accelerating, with the arms trailing, done by about 0.8 s; the weapon (the RPG if one was out) falls on its own path and lands clear of the body; final legs bent unevenly; a prone soldier slumps in place), culls by distance (650 m) and frustum, sorts near to far, poses each visible soldier (parameter set per pose, then FK for pelvis/torso/head, two-bone IK for arms and legs with pole vectors, hands placed on the weapon grips; upright feet follow the ground, lying bodies tilt to the slope) and packs 13 matrices plus `(heat,0,0,0)` per instance (`IST` = 212 floats) into `SOL.out`; no per-frame allocation, about 0.15 ms CPU for 40. `main.js` uploads that to a storage buffer and draws near instances then far instances (`firstInstance`) with `infPipe` (vertex `vsInf`, fragment `fs` of `object.wgsl`, back-face culled) in the main pass and with `infShPipe` (near mesh) in shadow cascades 0 and 1. Cost on a GTX 1060 with 40 soldiers in view: GPU time within noise of none (at most about 0.2 ms; `tools/infperf.mjs`). `tools/soldiercheck.mjs` checks triangle counts, winding against the normals and NaNs; `tools/soldierpose.mjs [aim] [deadTime]` (no GPU) measures the pose geometry: eye position in the rifle frame (cheek weld; the aim poses put it about 3 cm left of and 8 cm above the bore), butt to shoulder, support-hand gap, elbow and knee bends, the lowest limb point. Gotchas: a positive torso/head roll tilts the top to the left (the cheek-weld lean is negative roll); in prone (pelvis pitched −π/2) the torso's P[8] roll is the turn about the vertical and P[6] rolls the body about its long axis.
- **Infantry logic (`infantry.js`, phase 6 part B):** `initInfantry(C, G, helpers)` is called at the end of `initCombat`. Interface with the renderer (part A): `G.inf = { n, d }` is created in `initCombat` (`MAXI` = 40 from `infantry.js`); per soldier 8 floats at `i*8` in `d`: `x y z` (feet), `yaw` (vehicle convention, forward = (−sin, 0, −cos)), `pose` (0 stand, 1 walk, 2 run, 3 kneel-aim, 4 prone-aim, 5 stand-aim, 6 dead, 7 RPG-aim), `phase` (animation clock in s; for the dead: seconds since death), `aim` (weapon pitch, up positive), `state` (0 unused, 1 alive, 2 dead); `n` is the upper bound of used slots. The logic writes them every tick and allocates nothing per frame. Squads (`QK`: 0 RPG team, 1 ATGM team, 2 dismounts; up to `MAXQ` = `MAXI`), soldier roles rifleman / RPG / ATGM gunner / loader, behaviour modes hide / move / fire / flee, staggered line of sight every 0.45 s per soldier. Perception uses combat's `losVis` and `playerSig` (observer `night` 0.75) with a short range scale (`SEE_RI` 420 m; the ATGM sight 900 m), and they hear a moving tank inside 150 m; one who sees tells the squad. RPG teams (2–3) are placed at a wave start (`inf.wave` after `releaseWave`; from wave 2, 2–3 in the final wave) 120–350 m out on the main axis: in an intact house near a road (just inside the wall facing the player, with an eye point at the window 0.9 m outside it, `EX/EY/EZ`; hidden soldiers inside a house cannot be hit or seen from far, only at the window while aiming) or prone at a forest edge. They fire inside 250 m once they see the tank: shell type 12 (RPG rocket, 120 m/s, the motor cancels 80 % of gravity, grey trail in `trail()`, back-blast, 1.2 s aim, 8 s reload), 20 / 28 / 35 damage front / side / rear (`hitVehicle`). A building state change (`bldState` → `inf.onBld`): collapse kills those inside, damage kills half and scatters the rest (out of action for ~30 s); whenever soldiers lose their house binding (damage, a withdrawal at the end of a wave, an RPG out of rockets) they first step out through the nearest wall (`leave`: the buildings' push), so none is ever a soldier inside a wall. ATGM teams (gunner kneeling with the launcher, loader prone) on a height 380–950 m out (the 'ATGM ambush', the final wave, sometimes from wave 4) fire shell type 8; `S[o+9] < 0` marks a soldier as the guiding launcher (`guide()` asks `inf.lpos`). Dismounts: a BMP or BTR (`e.dsm` once) that stops in contact (engaging, |v| < 1, < 400 m; or standing still for 4 s within 250 m) drops 4–6 men behind it (one RPG when 5+); they deploy, then advance by bounds (alternate fire teams rush 20–30 m to cover: forest or the lee of a wall, avoiding the own minefields) to a stand-off of 100–160 m, kneeling or prone in between, and fire 3-round bursts of shell type 9. MG near misses pin soldiers down (`SUP`: prone, crawl, no fire above 3); a squad down to a third, or every squad when a wave is cleared, withdraws about 330 m (`flee`) and is freed once 300 m from the player and the camera (or at the next wave start when 200 m away). RPG accuracy: aim error 0.5 m + 1.2 % of the range, so about half the rockets miss a still tank at 250 m. Damage: player rounds are tested per frame segment against capsules (`seg`, r 0.3 × 1.8 m standing, 1.17 m kneeling, a lying 1.6 m capsule r 0.22 prone) before the sub-steps (APFSDS flies on); `splash()` with owner 0 calls `inf.blast` (kill radius max(4, 0.8 r), × 0.6 prone, × 0.5 in a house; pins down to 3×); the tracks kill (hull footprint while moving or turning; a still tank pushes them aside). The dead stay; a slot is recycled only after 20 s and 150 m from the camera. 10 points per soldier, 40 for an RPG or ATGM team (toast). The crew spots soldiers like vehicles but at shorter range (600 / 380 / 220 m standing / kneeling / prone, × 1.6 with the thermal sight, × 0.12 hidden in a house), drones see exposed ones within 0.6 × their range; spotted soldiers are small squares on the minimap (`inf.map`, yellow for RPG/ATGM, grey dead). The sight ray ranges on exposed soldiers (`G.aimHit`). Test hooks: `__game.infSpawn(role, x, z[, building])` (`rifle rpg atgm loader`), `__game.infSquad(kind, x, z, n, building)` (`rpg atgm dis auto-rpg auto-atgm`; for `dis` x is the enemy vehicle, for `auto-*` x is the bearing), `__game.inf` (arrays `sq role mode see vis spot seen hb sup rel`, counters `kills rockets atgms bursts drawn`, `reset()`).
