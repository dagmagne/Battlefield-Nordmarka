# Missions and the camouflage net

`missions.js` (stages, script API, the six missions, cards) and the camouflage net (`net.js`). Part of the developer notes indexed in `CLAUDE.md`; update this file when the system changes.

- **Missions (`missions.js`):** `MISSIONS` is the table (id, name, `card` text for the start screen, `brief`, `lvl` standing in for the wave number, `tod`, `arty` fire missions, `start` [x, z] snapped to the nearest road or trail) and `initMissions(C, G, h)` the engine (called from `main.js` after `initCombat`; it wraps `G.update` and `G.restart` like `support.js`; `window.__msn`). While a mission runs `G.msn` is set, `G.state` is 1 and combat.js skips its own wave block; `G.msnHud` is the top pill's text, `G.msnMap` draws the objective on both maps, `G.msnDead` words the knocked-out card. A mission is a list of **stages**: `t` objective text, `zone` [x, z, r, kind] (0 drive there, 1 hold, 2 enemy there, 3 search area), `cnt` (tag whose live vehicles the pill counts), `on(m)` setup, `ev` [[seconds into the stage, fn]], `done(m)`, and the ways to lose: `limit` (mission clock, s), `cap` [x, z, r, s] (live enemy vehicles inside for s seconds in all), `esc` (vehicles with a goal that reach it are removed and counted) with `fail(m)`. Checks run four times a second. The script API `m`: `veh(type, role, x, z, { tag, gar, goal, via, glr, hold, k, step })` (`via`: waypoints driven in order, advanced within 70 m; the column needs them, because with one far goal `pathDir` gave up where the road bends away and the column split over other roads and the forest) (road-bound types and columns (`k`) stand on the nearest road point, the rest on open ground found with `G.okSpot`), `rpg(x, z, n)` (in the nearest intact house within 70 m, else lying in the open), `atgm`, `mines` (a field across the nearest road or trail), `air(type, bearing)`, `shell()` (an enemy barrage on the tank's position), `chase(type)`, `left(tag)`, `seen(tag)`, `inZone()`, `resupply()`, `say`. **Hooks in combat.js:** `spawn(type, role, brg, grp, fx, fz, fyaw)` takes a fixed place (`G.lastSpawn` is the vehicle); `e.tag`; `e.gar` (garrison: stands still until it sees the tank, is hit, the tank is within 140 m, or a contact is reported within 700 m); `e.gl/glx/glz/glr` (a goal on the map instead of the player: `roleGoal` uses it, and the vehicle halts within `glr`; with `hold: 0` it does not stop to fight, as the column). **The six** (all on the real world; placements are printed by `missiontest`): `contact` First contact (reach the Øvresetertjern car park, a patrol of four, then a T-72 with a BMP), `convoy` Column on Holmenkollveien (eight vehicles drive road 4 from the arena to Voksenkollen along the `HKV` waypoints in about three minutes; at most two may get through; a wreck in the underpass at the arena blocks those behind it, which is fair play), `tower` Retake the tower (garrison of five with an RPG team, an ATGM team and mines; drive up; hold against five from the west and the south, lost after 40 s of enemy control), `arena` Hold the arena (three attacks of 4, 5 and 6 vehicles with drones, resupply between, lost after 45 s of enemy control), `cp` Find the command post (blue hour, two fire missions; the post stands at one of four sites on the eastern forest road (`CPS`, picked at random; `__msn.force = i` fixes it for tests) inside a 1000 m search zone, under camouflage nets: `e.cam` 0.24 scales the range at which a drone camera or the crew makes a vehicle out, so the recon drone sees it from about 350 m instead of 1500 (the owner found the fixed, uncamouflaged post was seen the moment the drone was up); found when one of its three vehicles has been seen; its fire only searches: `m.shell()` lands a barrage 90–180 m off the tank (250–400 m off a netted one) about every two minutes and not at all while a crew or an Orlan observes the tank, and an Orlan comes looking at 150 s and 400 s, so accurate fire needs the normal 15 s of observation in `arty.js`. The first version aimed every barrage at the tank's exact position: the owner sat under the net flying a drone and was killed by the second barrage without ever being seen), `night` Night breakthrough (night; seven vehicles, an RPG and an ATGM team and three minefields in four ambushes along 3 km; pursuers after five minutes; 14 minutes). **UI:** the objective pill `#obj` (text, what is left, clock, range and compass direction to the zone; rebuilt only when an integer key changes), a dashed ring and a pin on the minimap and big map, and a 24 m column of coloured smoke (particle kind 1; green: drive there, blue: hold) at a place to reach or hold. Cards go through `ui.card({ title, text, cont, go, goFn, alt, altFn, keys })` (`ui.over` is a wrapper): the briefing (nothing is placed until it is closed; skipped with `nohint`), mission complete (next mission / play again; the best time per mission is kept in `localStorage.gmsn` and shown on the start screen) and mission failed. `api.mission(id)` switches mission without a reload ('' = the defence waves); peace mode switches a mission off. The menu row Game has Restart (touch had no restart outside the end card) and the way back to the start screen. Not tuned by play: every balance number (group sizes, the clocks, 40 / 45 s of control) is a first guess, verified only to work mechanically.
- **Camouflage net (K, touch NET `#bN`, pad hold □ (a tap still changes ammunition, now on release; the touchpad click was tried first, but the browser treats the DualSense touchpad as a mouse, so it moved the sight and fired); `G.netKey`, `G.netOn`, `G.net` 0..1; `src/net.js`):** the owner's idea after the command post: something to rig before flying a drone, that works against being found but not in a fight. The crew rigs it in `NET_T` = 8 s on a still tank (|v| < 0.5; the `#sN` pill counts down, then turns green); driving (|v| > 1), the main gun and either MG throw it off at once, K stows it; flying a drone leaves it up; a restart clears it. Effect, only while fully up: an observer that has **not** detected the tank yet sees `NETK` = 0.35 of its signature (enemy vehicles: `e.see < 1` in `updateEnemy`; soldiers: `SEE[i] < 1` in `infantry.js`), a searching FPV or Lancet gets 0.35 of its lock chance, and an Orlan that is not already watching does not find it at all (visibility × 0.24 against its 0.25 threshold; 0.45 at first, which still found a netted tank in the open). Anything that has detected the tank (`see` >= 1), a diving drone and an Orlan already watching are not affected. Measured on the lake (`nettest`, with `SEEK` 0.6): a T-72 with a clear line detects a still tank in 2.5 / 3 s at 150 / 250 m without the net and 12.5 / 15.5 s with it; at 350 m with a partly blocked line 19.5 s against never; by the formula a netted tank in the open is not detected beyond about 520 m (1400 m without). **Model:** `buildNet()` lays three draped height fields as flat-shaded quads in the tank's part spaces (0 hull, 1 turret, 2 gun; about 1300 triangles), so `main.js` draws it with a copy of the Leopard's part matrices (`netO`; main pass and shadow passes, hidden in the gunner sight like the tank) and it follows a traversed turret; while it is rigged the matrices' y columns are scaled, so it rises from the ground. `MAT.net` 33 in `object.wgsl`: white, grey and a little olive patches from the vertex colour, creases, and garnish holes cut with `discard` (closed beyond 25–70 m, where they would shimmer); cool (0.2) in the thermal sight. Antennas, the MG and the cupola poke through it. Not done: the net does not hide the tank from the player's own map or change what the enemy artillery already knows; its shaded side gets no snow-bounce fill and is rather dark.
