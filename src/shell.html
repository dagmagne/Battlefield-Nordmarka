RECON (2): climbs to 110 m, 6 min, unarmed; what its camera sees goes on the map; X with it up hands it to its autopilot (it circles and keeps watching until the battery is flat) and puts you back in the tank. While it watches you can pick another type with Z and launch that; with RECON picked, X takes the watching drone back. X again cuts the link (the drone is lost).
